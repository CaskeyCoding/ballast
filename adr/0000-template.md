# ADR 0000: Template

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXXX. (Accepted for the records in this folder.)

## Context

The forces at play: what problem this decision addresses, the constraints (cost, time, the standing
fleet conventions), and what is true about the system that makes a choice necessary.

## Options

The alternatives considered, each with its trade-off. Name the ones rejected, not only the winner,
so the reasoning is reviewable later.

## Decision

The choice made, stated plainly in one or two sentences.

## Consequences

What this buys and what it costs: the follow-on obligations, the things now easy, the things now
harder, and the seam left open for a future change.
