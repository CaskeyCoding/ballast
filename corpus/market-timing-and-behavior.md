# Market Timing and Behavior

Market timing is the attempt to buy and sell based on predictions of short-term market moves. It is
very difficult to do consistently, because markets are hard to forecast and a large share of an
investment's gains can come from a small number of strong days. An investor who is out of the market
trying to avoid a downturn can miss those days and end up worse off.

Emotions are a common source of investing mistakes. Fear can lead to panic selling after prices have
already fallen, locking in losses, while excitement can lead to chasing investments that have
recently risen. Both reactions tend to buy high and sell low, the opposite of what investors intend.

Several habits help counter these tendencies. Setting a plan based on goals and time horizon,
staying diversified, and investing regularly through approaches like dollar-cost averaging reduce
the pull of short-term emotion. It also helps to remember that past performance does not predict
future results.
